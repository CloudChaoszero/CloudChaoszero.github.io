## Self-Reflection

In the past 5 years, I continued growth in my career by leading and delivering results in a smaller company after being impacted by an April 2025 RIF at my prior company. It was a blessing in disguise where I had to scratch the itch of driving more impact within innovation of GenAI and my field. Moreover, I had delivered in advocating for diverse dense housing in rich resourced areas--placing gentrification in other neighborhoods, not just low resourced ones. Lastly, I became a teaching instructor for Urban Cycling at another nonprofit as well. In that path were underlying 2010, 2016, and 2021 5-year goals, graduating from college, getting a career, and also being engaged! Of course, these ambitions and goals were certainly never expected of me, and I had to work to where I am now.

How so? Well, in:

* ~2009: I had the opportunity to drop out of High School, without traditional 'sit-com family confrontational' repercussions I used to watch.

* 2010: The Great Recession lead me to not finding a job after High School (I was looking for warehouse or Fast Food jobs). So, I enrolled into Junior College, not knowing what to do.
  * 2013: I transferred to UC Davis--truly thankful of their diversity initiatives for accepting me.

* ~2016: I had the opportunity to save up ~2months worth of rent and take a [strategic risk to move to San Francisco](https://raulingaverage.dev/i-wrote-this-when-i-was-a-teller-at-wells-fargo-in-2016/), or move back to Modesto.

> “As a child, I was always one level below the average in math, and I felt incompetent compared to peers.” --[My "5-Year Plan For Career Success" feature in a Udacity blog post](https://blog.udacity.com/2017/12/5-year-plan-for-career-success.html)

* ~2021: I met my partner and now are living together in the Bay Area! Building on our separate foundations and continuing to support one another in our own strategic risks.

**Reiterating**, I had several opportunities to regress into an expected lifestyle, even so without a safety net. However, with lot of preparation, grit, network of opportunities & people, **someone to personally help me in my journey**, I am fortunately still able to continue with my goals.

## Motivation for this Roadmap

The above prelude is not a boast, flex, or brag. But rather, a reflection on some thoughts I had in the past couple of years, especially this one...
* Growing my Chicano Roots - How can I still support volunteering my time to advocacy in Housing affordability, Sustainable Transportation, and helping those around me? Moreover, synthesizing this beyond non-spanish (i.e. "No Sabo") speaking experience?
* How can my partner and myself prepare to form a family?
* How can I AI-proof my data profession in a rapid growing (Gen)AI space?

I am curious about being in this dicotomy ofthe [k-shaped](https://www.cnet.com/personal-finance/americas-v-shaped-vs-k-shaped-economic-recovery-what-does-it-all-mean/) bifurcation of the economy, which has gotten worse after COVID. There are other things I think about (e.g. impact of 4th gen US citizens like myself and ICE and re-emergence of McCarthyism)...but I am taking that day by day...
